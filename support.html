Common questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Marlow. Apple handles billing and cancellation.

How do I delete my account, or my child's? In the app, a parent or tutor opens the Account menu on the dashboard, chooses Edit Profile, and then chooses Delete My Account. To delete a child's account, a parent opens that child's page and chooses Delete [child's name]'s Account. When a parent deletes their own account, the app shows the names of any children only that parent manages and asks them to confirm that those accounts will be deleted too. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription (see above). Safety reports are kept in anonymized form, without names or account links. You can also email privacy@learnwithmarlow.com from the parent's email address and we will help.

My child forgot their PIN, or is locked out. Child PINs are 6 digits. After several wrong tries a child's sign-in is locked for a short time. A parent can open the child's page and choose to reset the PIN, which gives a new 6-digit PIN. Older 4-digit PINs keep working until you reset them.

How does my child tell a grown-up about a problem? In a live help session, the child can tap Tell a grown-up. Parents can also file a full report about a tutor from the child's page in the app.

Can I change my name? Yes. Open the Account menu, choose Edit Profile, change your name and tap Save.

Something isn't working. Tell us what you were doing, which iPhone or iPad you have, and what you saw on screen.
