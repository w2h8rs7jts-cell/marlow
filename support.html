Common questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Marlow. Apple handles billing and cancellation.

How do I delete my account, or my child's? In the app, a parent or tutor opens the Account menu on the dashboard and chooses Delete My Account. To delete a child's account, a parent opens that child's page and chooses Delete [child's name]'s Account. You can also email privacy@learnwithmarlow.com from the parent's email address and we will help.

Something isn't working. Tell us what you were doing, which iPhone or iPad you have, and what you saw on screen.
