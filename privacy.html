Marlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who we are and who this covers

Marlow is a homework and reading helper for children, run by John Archuleta (Marlow), 2880 Bicentennial Pkwy, Ste 100 #1090, Henderson, NV 89044. Questions about privacy go to privacy@learnwithmarlow.com.

This policy covers the Marlow app for iPhone and iPad and the servers behind it. Four kinds of people use Marlow: parents, children (accounts a parent creates), tutors, and staff of partner libraries.

How we use information

We use information only to run Marlow:

  • Provide the features: scanning homework, step-by-step help, lessons, reading, spelling and quizzes, and tutoring sessions.
  • Show parents their child's progress.
  • Run subscriptions and library access.
  • Keep children safe: review help requests and misconduct reports, pause a tutor's connection to a child while a serious report is reviewed, and have a person review every report and decide what happens next.
  • Fix problems and keep the service secure.

We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use children's information for advertising or tracking.

Children's privacy

Marlow is made for children, and a child cannot create an account alone. A parent creates the child's account and, by doing so, consents to the collection described in this policy. Before the account can be created, the parent must confirm in the app that they are the child's parent or legal guardian and agree to this policy.

We collect only what the child's lessons need. Children's information is never sold, never used for advertising and never made public.

A parent can:

  • Review their child's information and progress in the parent dashboard.
  • Ask us to correct or delete their child's information.
  • Refuse any further collection and ask us to delete the child's account.

To do any of these, open your child's page in the app and use Delete [child's name]'s Account to delete the account and everything saved to it, or write to privacy@learnwithmarlow.com. If we learn we collected a child's information without a parent's consent, we will delete it.

Tutors, libraries and safety

Tutors are adults with their own accounts. A tutor can work with a child only after a parent approves the connection, or when a tutor from a partner library accepts a help request that the child or parent sent to that library. Tutor profiles, including credentials, are written by the tutor. Marlow does not verify them, and parents see them before approving a connection. Tutors are independent adults, not Marlow employees. Marlow does not set tutor rates, take a share of what a tutor charges, or handle payment between a family and a tutor. A tutor may list a website or payment page in their profile; a link opens outside Marlow, is not checked by Marlow, and is covered by that site's own policies.

A parent or child can report a tutor's conduct. The report's text goes to an AI model that sorts it by category and severity. Reports the AI rates high-severity automatically pause that tutor's connection to the child (and cancel any open help request with that tutor) until a person reviews the report; the AI never removes a tutor from Marlow or makes the final decision. A person reviews every report and decides what happens next. If the AI is unavailable, the report is still saved, no automatic pause is applied, and it is marked for human review. Library administrators can see reports about tutors in their own library.

During a live session, a child sees a Tell a grown-up button. Tapping it sends a message, with a snapshot of the shared whiteboard, to the child's approved parents so they can check in. We recommend that a parent stay nearby during live help.

Safety reports after an account is deleted. To protect other children, we keep safety and misconduct reports after the reporter's, the tutor's or the child's account is deleted. When that happens the report is anonymized: names and links to the deleted account are removed. What remains is the category, severity, date, status, the library involved (if any) and the written description, which may be kept for safety review. Because the description is free text, please avoid putting names in it.

Child sign-in protection. New child PINs are 6 digits, randomly generated, and weak PINs such as 123456 are not allowed. Repeated wrong PIN attempts temporarily lock that child's sign-in, and a parent can reset the PIN from the child's page. PINs created earlier with 4 digits keep working until a parent resets them.

Voice clips and whiteboard drawings in a session are visible to the people in that session.

Permissions, retention, security and contact

Device permissions. Marlow asks for the camera to scan homework, the microphone for Read to Marlow and for voice clips in tutoring sessions, and speech recognition for Read to Marlow. Each can be turned off in iOS Settings; the matching feature then stops working. Marlow shows reminders from the app itself on your device and does not use remote push notifications.

Retention. We keep information while the account is active. Parents and tutors can delete their own account at any time from their dashboard (Account, then Edit Profile, then Delete My Account), and a parent can delete a child's account from that child's page. When an account is deleted, its information is deleted, including homework photos and voice clips. When a parent deletes their own account, the app first shows the names of any children who have no other approved parent and asks the parent to confirm; those children's accounts and everything saved to them are then deleted too. Children who have another approved parent are not deleted. Safety reports are kept in anonymized form, as described above. Copies in routine backups may remain for a short time before they are overwritten. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription; that is managed in your Apple ID settings. Information stored only on your device, including any recovery copies kept there, is removed when you delete the app.

Security. Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with access rules that limit each person to their own family's records. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell parents in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
