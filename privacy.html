Who we are and who this covers

How we use information

We use information only to run Marlow:

  • Provide the features: scanning homework, step-by-step help, lessons, reading, spelling and quizzes, and tutoring sessions.
  • Show parents their child's progress.
  • Run subscriptions and library access.
  • Keep children safe: review help requests and misconduct reports, and suspend tutors when needed.
  • Fix problems and keep the service secure.

We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use children's information for advertising or tracking.

Children's privacy

Marlow is made for children, and a child cannot create an account alone. A parent creates the child's account and, by doing so, consents to the collection described in this policy. [Describe how parental consent is obtained and recorded.]

We collect only what the child's lessons need. Children's information is never sold, never used for advertising and never made public.

A parent can:

  • Review their child's information and progress in the parent dashboard.
  • Ask us to correct or delete their child's information.
  • Refuse any further collection and ask us to delete the child's account.

To do any of these, [describe the in-app steps] or write to privacy@learnwithmarlow.com. If we learn we collected a child's information without a parent's consent, we will delete it.

Tutors, libraries and safety

Tutors are adults with their own accounts. A tutor can work with a child only after the relationship is approved [confirm that a parent approves it]. [Explain what "verified" means for a tutor.]

A parent or child can report a tutor's conduct. The report's text goes to an AI model that sorts it by category and severity, and a high-severity report can suspend the tutor right away while it is reviewed. Library administrators can see reports about tutors in their own library.

Voice clips and whiteboard drawings in a session are visible to the people in that session.

Permissions, retention, security and contact

Device permissions. Marlow asks for the camera to scan homework, the microphone for Read Aloud and for voice clips in tutoring sessions, and speech recognition for Read Aloud. Each can be turned off in iOS Settings; the matching feature then stops working.

Security. Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with access rules that limit each person to their own family's records. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell parents in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
